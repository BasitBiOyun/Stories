## Chapter 1: The Cruel King

Moses (as) was a great Messenger of Allah. He lived in Egypt more than 3000 years ago. Pharaoh was the king of Egypt. He was an unfair and cruel ruler. He did not believe in Allah. He thought he was a god. There was a big group in Egypt: the Children of Israel. They were the grandchildren of Jacob (as) (Yakub) and Joseph (as) (Yûsuf). They believed in one Allah, like their great-grandfather, Prophet Abraham (as). So, they did not think the king was a god. This group was more crowded than the Egyptians. The king feared that this crowd took control of his kingdom. Because of his fear, he was rude to the Children of Israel. He made them unhappy. He forced them to do dangerous work for no money.

## Chapter 2: The King’s Dream

Years passed and many despotic kings ruled Egypt. One day the king had a dream. He saw a fire in his vision. The fire came from Jerusalem and burnt the houses of the Egyptians. But it did not harm any Children of Israel. The king woke up and called his helpers and magicians. He asked about the dream. They said: “A boy will be born from the Children of Israel and he will kill the Egyptians.” Then, the king told his soldiers: “Kill all the baby boys of the Children of Israel.” His soldiers entered the houses and searched the rooms. When they found baby boys, they killed them. All mothers, fathers, and sisters cried, but the soldiers had no kindness. They were unkind and heartless.

## Chapter 3: A Baby in a Basket

Moses was born in that year. His mother was afraid soldiers could find her baby. So, she could not sleep at night. She did not know what to do. She asked Allah for help. Allah said to her: “Take a basket. Put your baby in the basket. Take the basket to the River Nile. Allah will protect him.” Moses’s mother put the baby Moses in a basket and took it to the river. She put the basket in the water. The water carried it away. Moses’s mother was very worried and told her daughter to follow the basket. Moses’s mother was so sad. But she knew that Allah was kinder to the baby Moses than her. Allah was their Lord and also the Lord of the Nile.

## Chapter 4: The Queen’s Love

Allah told the waves to be kind while carrying the baby. Soon, the basket came to the waterside. It was near the king’s palace. The palace servants saw the basket and took it to the king and the queen, Asiye. Queen Asiye saw the baby, and Allah placed a lot of love for him in her heart. The queen was different from her husband. She believed in Allah. She was good and kind-hearted. She told her husband: “I will take him to the palace and look after him.” The king accepted. She said to her servants: “Find a nurse for the baby.” Moses’s sister saw everything. She ran to the Queen and said: “I know a good woman. She lives nearby. She will be a good nurse for the baby.”

## Chapter 5: Back to His Mother

Moses’s sister brought her mother to the palace to take care of the baby. Allah sent Moses back to his mother. Baby Moses was in his mother’s arms again. His mother looked after baby Moses. Allah is the Best Protector. Queen Asiye loved Moses so much. Moses grew up in a safe place, in the house of the king. He always loved Allah. He knew that the king was not god. Years passed and Moses became a strong young man. He was always ready to help the weak people. He loved the Children of Israel, because his mother was from the Children of Israel. One day he was walking in the bazaar. He saw two men fighting. One of them was an Egyptian.

## Chapter 6: A Mistake in the Bazaar

The other man was from the Children of Israel. Suddenly, a poor and weak man from the Children of Israel shouted: “Help me!” Moses ran to help him. He hit the Egyptian and the man died there. Moses was very upset. He was so sorry, because he killed a man by mistake. He made dua to Allah: “Forgive me! I killed a man. I didn’t do it on purpose, it was an accident.” Allah forgave him because He is Kind and Loving. A friend of Moses came and said to him: “The king’s soldiers are looking for you. You killed an Egyptian. Now they are going to catch you. Run away from the city. Soldiers of the king will kill you.”

## Chapter 7: Escape to Midian

Moses knew that the penalty for killing an Egyptian was death. Moses escaped from the soldiers. He left Egypt and travelled for a long time. He was not alone. Allah guided him. After many days, he arrived at Midian (Medyen). Midian was near Egypt. The king of Egypt was not the ruler there. Moses was tired and thirsty. So, he looked for water. Soon, he found a well. There were many men around the water. These were shepherds with their sheep. They were watering their animals. The two girls were sitting far from the water. They also had sheep with them. But they did not go near the well. Moses asked the girls: “Why are you sitting far away from the well?”

## Chapter 8: The Two Sisters

“Why aren’t you taking your sheep to drink water?” The girls said: “Our father is very old. He cannot come with us. There are no young men in our house to help us. So, we take the sheep to the water. We wait for our turn.” Moses understood the two sisters needed help. He took their sheep to the water and gave them a drink. The girls were very happy. They took their sheep and went home. When the sisters came home early, their father asked: “What happened? You came home very early!” The sisters said: “A young man helped us to water our sheep. We did not wait for the other shepherds to leave the well.”

## Chapter 9: A New Life

The father of these girls was Prophet Şuayb (as). Şuayb (as) was a Messenger of Allah in Midian. He was very old, so he couldn’t help his daughters. Şuayb (as) said: “Go and call the young man, I want to thank him for his work.” Moses came to the house. He ate food with the girls’ father. Şuayb (as) offered Moses a job. Moses accepted the job, because he was a stranger in Midian. He needed a job and a house. Moses began to stay with the family. He helped them in their work. Later, he married one of the sisters. They lived a happy life. After ten years, Moses decided to go back to Egypt.

## Chapter 10: The Voice on the Mountain

He took his family. They started to travel to Egypt. They travelled a long time. It was winter. There was nobody on the way. It was getting dark and cold. They were in a valley between mountains. Suddenly, Moses saw a fire on the hillside. He said to his family: “Wait here. I will get some fire to get warm.” Moses climbed the mountain. It was already dark. Everywhere was very quiet. In the silence he heard a loud voice: “Moses, I am Allah. I chose you. Listen to me. There is no god but Allah.” Then Allah told Moses: “Put down your walking stick!” Moses put down his walking stick on the ground. It turned into a big snake!

## Chapter 11: The Signs of Allah

Allah spoke to Moses again: “Put your hand into your chest. Now take it out. See, your hand is now shining.” Allah said: “I am showing you the signs of My power. Go to the king and tell him Allah is the most powerful.” After this message from Allah, Moses became a Messenger of Allah, a prophet. Moses (as) went to Egypt. He met the king and his helpers. But they did not believe in Allah. Allah chose Moses and made him His prophet. Moses (as) took his brother Harun with him and went to the palace to give the message of Allah. The king was sitting on his throne. His helpers and soldiers were standing around him.

## Chapter 12: The King’s Magicians

Moses (as) told him about Allah. He said to him: “I am the Messenger from the Lord of the universe.” The king listened to Moses (as). He thought Moses was crazy. Moses (as) decided to show the miracles. Moses (as) took his walking stick and threw it on the ground. The stick turned into a big snake! Then he put his arm in his armpit. He got out his arm. Look! It was shining white! These were miracles of Allah. Allah gave them to Moses (as) to help him. The king and his helpers laughed at him. He made fun of Moses and Allah. The king said to Moses (as): “This is only magic. All of my magicians can do this.”

## Chapter 13: The Magicians Believe

He invited his magicians to the palace. The magicians came with sticks in their hands. Some were holding ropes. They put down the sticks and ropes on the ground. They were moving like snakes! Then, Allah spoke to Moses (as): “Throw your stick!” Moses (as) threw down his stick and it turned into a huge snake. His stick quickly ate all the snakes of the magicians. The magicians bowed down in front of Moses (as). They shouted: “We believe in the Lord of Moses and Harun!” The king still did not believe in Allah, because he was arrogant. The king did not like those people because they believed in Allah. He continued to harm Moses (as) and his people day and night.

## Chapter 14: The Night Journey

He told his helpers and soldiers: “Be rude to them!” Life became harder for them. Moses (as) asked Allah for help. And Allah told him: “Travel with your people at night.” Moses (as) called his people and said: “Get ready for the journey. We are going away from Egypt. But you must keep it secret. We must hide it. We will leave at night.” They left Egypt at night. There was a large group with many old people and children. The children and the old could not walk fast and got tired very quickly. Their caravan moved slowly. When the sun rose, they arrived at the sea. The king prepared his huge army.

## Chapter 15: The Sea Opens

Moses (as) and his people saw the king and his soldiers following them. His people panicked. But Moses (as) said: “Calm down! Allah is with us and will help us go to a safe place.” Soon, the king and his soldiers caught them! The sea was in front of them. The king and his army were behind them. They had no place to escape. But Allah was with them. He told Moses (as): “Hit the sea with your stick!” He hit the sea and the sea parted! The waves became high walls! They could see the sandy ground! Allah made a road in the sea for them. Moses (as) and his people safely walked between the walls of water.

## Chapter 16: The Cruel King Died in the Water

The king and his soldiers were very close behind them. They also saw the road in the middle of the sea. The king said to his soldiers: “Look! I told the sea to open, and it opened.” They entered the waters. When they were midway, Allah closed the sea over them. In the end, they died in the water. The story of Moses (as) has many lessons to learn. It tells us that no one can own another person. It shows that only Allah has power over us.
