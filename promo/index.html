Adam (pbuh) is the first Messenger and the father of all humans. Allah created him from soil and showed him great respect and gave him full value as the first human. The Holy Qur’an tells his tale in different surahs. Surahs A’raf, Baqarah, Hicr, Isra, Sâd and Taha describe Adam (pbuh)’s tale clearly. As the grandchildren of Adam (pbuh), we can learn many lessons from this fabulous but true story. In this story, there are numerous important messages about humans’ role in life.

After Allah created the sky and the earth, He told the angels that He was going to create a human. He said He had decided to place a ruler (halîfe) on earth. This ruler would live there for many years. The angels were surprised and began to wait with curiosity.
