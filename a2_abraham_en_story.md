# Story of Prophet Abraham (AS)

## Chapter 1 - Abraham and the Idols

STORY OF PROPHET ABRAHAM (AS)

A very long time ago, a boy was born in the kingdom of Babylon in Mesopotamia. His parents named him Abraham. Abraham was an honest and intelligent boy. He loved Allah more than anything. When he grew up, Allah made him His great Messenger. People in Abraham’s town did not know Allah. They did not worship Him. They bowed to the stars, the moon, the sun, and stone objects. These objects looked like people or animals.

People believed these objects were their gods. They even made a special house for them. They came to the house, talked to the stone gods, and made a wish. Abraham thought that was silly. He was a smart boy. He saw that the stone objects did not eat, drink, or talk. They could not move from one place to another, and they could not hear or understand their wishes!

## Chapter 2 - Abraham and His Father

Abraham’s father was an idol maker. As a young boy, Abraham used to watch his father making idols from stone. One day, Abraham asked his father: “Why do you make and put these toys in the house?” His father said, “They are not toys. They are our gods. We show love and respect to them. We ask them for help and give them presents.”

But Abraham played with these idols as toys. He rode on their backs and sometimes kicked them. One day, his father saw Abraham. He was riding the king of the gods. He got angry and told his son not to play with it again. Abraham asked, “What is this human-like object, father? It has big ears. His ears are bigger than ours.” His father answered, “It is Mardukh, the king of gods, son! He has big ears because he is very smart.” This made Abraham laugh; that was so ridiculous.

## Chapter 3 - Abraham Looks at the Sky

He was only seven years old at that time. Years passed and Abraham grew up. He always thought about Allah. He wanted to know who created the World. He knew that Allah was not an object. He was sad to see the people in the kingdom; they were still worshipping stone gods. Abraham was in search of one true Creator.

One night, Abraham left his home and took a walk in the countryside. In a nearby mountain, he found a cave and sat there. He started thinking about Allah. Then, he stood up and looked at the beautiful sky. He saw a bright star and thought, “Could this be my Allah?” But when it disappeared, he said, “I will not show respect to it, because it sets and disappears.” He then saw the shining moon and said, “Could this be Allah?” But when the moon went away, he said: “Allah never goes away!” He understood that it could not be Allah.

## Chapter 4 - Allah Guides Abraham

Abraham stayed all night. In the morning the sun came out. It was shining and very bright. When he saw the bright sun, he thought, “Could this be Allah? It is bigger and bright.” But when it set, he got the idea that Allah is not a creation. Allah is the Creator of everything. He takes care of us. He never leaves us alone.

Abraham put his forehead to the ground and spoke to Allah. He said, “If Allah doesn’t show me the right way, I will be on the wrong path.” Allah answered his prayer and made him His Messenger. By now, he had to guide his people. Prophet Abraham (AS) went home and told his father, “O my father, follow me: I will guide you on the right way. Stop worshipping stones.” His father got angry and said, “Are you against my gods?”

## Chapter 5 - Abraham Calls His People

“If you do not stop speaking like this, I will stone you. Leave here now.” Prophet Abraham (AS) was very sad for his father. Then, he went to the people of the kingdom. He was trying to show them the right way: “O people! I believe in Allah. I do not worship stone idols, because Allah is the one and only God!” People were very angry when they heard him.

Abraham (AS) said to them, “Why do you bow down to these objects? They have no power to help you.” People said, “We saw our fathers worship them; because of this, we do the same.” Abraham (AS) told them, “My Allah gives me food and drink when I need them. He heals me when I am sick. Your gods have no power to do these.” But people didn’t listen to him.

## Chapter 6 - Abraham Breaks the Idols

Abraham (AS) wanted to show his people that it was so silly to love and talk to idols. He made a plan to break all their stone gods. But he did not tell anyone about his plan. There was a big festival soon. All the people went outside of town. He got an axe and waited. When the town was empty, he went into the big house full of human-like objects.

He saw all of them. They stood there. There were plates of food in front of them. Abraham (AS) jokingly asked them, “Why don’t you eat the food? It is getting cold.” Abraham (AS) began to break the stone gods, one after another. In the end, they were all broken. He left the largest one unbroken. Abraham (AS) placed the axe around its neck and hurried back home.

## Chapter 7 - The People Question Abraham

The next day, people went to the house of gods. They were shocked, because all their stone gods were in pieces. People came together around the pieces. They wondered and asked: “Who did this?” Some of them said: “We heard a young man; he spoke against our gods.” They remembered, “His name was Abraham.”

People found Abraham (AS) and brought him to the house of idols. They asked him: “Did you harm our gods?” Abraham (AS) calmly said, “It was this god there, the biggest one broke all the stones. Ask it, if it can speak!” The people were displeased with his answer and said, “You know that these objects don’t speak!” Abraham (AS) said, “Then, why do you worship these objects? Don’t you see that they can’t speak or see and even protect themselves? Are you crazy?”

## Chapter 8 - Abraham Is Thrown into the Fire

They felt ashamed and looked at each other, because they knew Abraham was right. But they were too arrogant to accept their mistake. They started shouting, “Burn him! Burn him! In the name of our gods, punish him!” People of the kingdom collected firewood for the fire for days. The fire was so huge that it was dangerous to go near it. But Abraham (AS) stayed calm, because he trusted Allah. He was always with him.

News about the fire travelled very fast and far. People from many different towns came to watch Abraham (AS). The fire was finally ready. It was so hot. Birds could not fly over the flames. People of the kingdom tied Abraham’s hands and feet. He placed him on a catapult. A catapult is a machine. People used this machine to throw Abraham (AS) into the fire.

## Chapter 9 - Allah Saves Abraham

They threw him into huge flames. At that moment, Angel Gabriel (Cebrâil) came to him and asked, “Is there anything you wish for?” Abraham (AS) only said, “Nothing from you!” He knew Allah was on his side. Allah said to the fire to be cool for Abraham (AS). The fire became cool and safe for him. It only burnt the ropes on Abraham’s (AS) hands and feet.

He sat in the middle of the fire safely. The fire turned into a garden. Prophet Abraham (AS) came out in good condition when the flames cooled. People were shocked to see that Abraham (AS) was not harmed at all. The miracle made the people amazed, but they did not change their mind. They were still arrogant and rude. Abraham (AS) tried hard to show them the truth, but they were still against him.

## Chapter 10 - Abraham and Nimrod

Nimrod was the King of Babylon. He heard about the miracle. He thought Abraham was not an ordinary person. So, he wanted to meet him. He called him and asked, “Who is your God?” Abraham (AS) said, “He is Allah, the One. He gives life and brings death.” “I can give life and death,” said Nimrod. He ordered his guards to bring two slaves.

Nimrod said: “Kill one of the slaves.” The guards killed one of them. Then Nimrod said, “I let the second slave live, let him go.” The slave was free. “See! I can also give life and death!” Nimrod said to Abraham (AS). Abraham (AS) said, “Allah makes the sun rise in the east. Can you make the sun rise from the west?” Of course, Nimrod couldn’t do this; only Allah can make the sun rise from the east. This made Nimrod more angry.

## Chapter 11 - Abraham Leaves Babylon

Only one woman and one man believed in Allah with Abraham (AS). The woman’s name was Sarah (Sâre), and the man’s name was Lot (Lût). Lot later became a prophet. Abraham (AS) understood that no one was going to listen to him. So, he decided to leave Babylon and travel to other lands to tell people about Allah’s message.

Abraham (AS) began his journey. He traveled from Babylon to Syria and Palestine on camels. It was a long, hot, and tiring journey. During his journey, Abraham (AS) married Hagar (Hacer) and had a son. They named the baby Ishmael (İsmâil). One day, Allah asked Abraham (AS) to travel with his wife and the little child, Ishmael. They all travelled for a long time. Finally, they arrived at a quiet valley near two small hills, Safa and Marwah.

## Chapter 12 - Hagar and Ishmael in the Valley

Abraham (AS) told his wife to stay near one of the hills with Ishmael. Abraham (AS) left them there. Hagar knew that this was part of Allah's plan. She thought fearlessly, “Allah will never let us die; He will surely protect us.” Allah is the best planner. The valley had no fruit, no trees, no food, and no water.

Abraham (AS) went away and asked Allah to protect his family. He asked Allah to give them blessings, so they could thank Him. But soon, Hagar and the little child Ishmael had no food and water left. Hagar had to give food to her child. She looked for food and water. She ran from hill to hill. But it was useless, there was no water and nobody to help her.

## Chapter 13 - The Zamzam Water

Hagar was running in the desert and Ishmael was crying for water. Suddenly water came out of the ground under Ishmael’s feet. When Hagar saw this, she shouted, “Zamzam!” It means “Flow slowly, stop!” She was so happy. She drank the water. Then she took some water in her hands and gave it to Ishmael.

Later, this water became very famous. It is still there today. The water is special because it was a gift from Allah in the middle of the desert. Ishmael and his mother began to live near the Zamzam water. More people came there because of this water. They built a city called Mecca.

## Chapter 14 - The Ka'ba and Abraham's Message

During that time, Abraham (AS) visited Mecca several times to see his family. One day, Allah asked Abraham (AS) to build the House of Allah, the Ka’ba. Abraham (AS) said to Ishmael, “O Ishmael, Allah gave me an important job, and you will help me with it.” Ishmael said, “I will help you for sure.” The Ka’ba was a very old building. It was the first holy place on Earth, but it was lost after a long time.

Father and son found the bottom of the old building of the Ka’ba and built the new building on it. After Abraham (AS) built the Holy Ka’ba, his mission was over. He built a special place for Muslims of all colors. Today, people still visit the House of Allah to make Hajj. Hajj makes us remember Abraham (AS) and his family's story.

After many years, Ishmael’s children had children. One of them was Muhammad, the Prophet of Islam (AS). Muhammad (AS) told people the message of his great-grandfather, Abraham (AS).

The message is: “There is no god but Allah. He has no partner, rival or helper. Allah is unique in every way.”
