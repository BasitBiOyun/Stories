# Ibrahim (abraham): matching audit for A2 and B1, EN and AR

Scope: every `matching` in the Language Focus sets for A2 and B1, in both EN and AR (B2 not covered). There are 106 in total: A2 EN 14, A2 AR 14, B1 EN 39, B1 AR 39.
Purpose codes: VOC = vocabulary-meaning, GRAM = grammar-form→meaning/function, DISC = discourse/linker/time-sequencing function, STRUCT = sentence structure / verb pattern, COLL = collocation, CAT = categorization-in-disguise, REG = register/text-type.
Word bank = gap-fill with word bank. Choose-the-form = inline MC inside the chapter sentence. The correct answer is shown in [brackets] and is listed first; the options should be shuffled when built.

Current layout: every A2 chapter is MA + two other types + Reflection, with the matching always in slot 1. Every B1 chapter is MA-MA-MA-Reflection.
