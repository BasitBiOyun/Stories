## Data problems (verified against src/data/abraham/b2/{en,ar}/pages.ts)
- EN ch2 cause-result: lefts "Due to the development or worsening of human capacity…" and "…were necessary" are not in the EN text ("As human conditions and capacities changed over time, some changes in details became necessary"). They are translations of the AR text.
- EN ch6 comparison-effect: "the god of gods" is not in the EN text ("Chief God of Babylon"); AR has «إله الآلهة».
- EN ch21 passive-change: "The catapult was shot" but the text says "was fired".
- EN ch29 source-voice: "symbolized by all Muslims in Hajj" but the text says "commemorated by Muslims during Hajj" (AR «رمزًا» does exist).
- AR ch20: «واتّفقوا على أنْ يَحْرِق إبراهيم حيًّا» (text + item) reads as "that Abraham burns". Fix: «أن يُحرَقَ إبراهيمُ» / «أن يَحرِقوا إبراهيمَ».
- AR ch10: «مُجْعَلَة» is non-standard (→ «مَجْعُولَة»), in both the text and the item.
- AR ch9: the "verse" is a paraphrase in «» (Qur'an 6:77 is «لئن لم يهدني ربي لأكونن…»). The item «فـ + سأكون» is a meta-fragment.
- Duplicate or overlapping lefts: ch7 (the "as if…" line appears in both matchings), ch22 (the "smoke had blackened" line appears in both), ch9 (items 3–4 are substrings of items 1–2), ch27-3 ("had commanded him" appears twice), ch30-3 (4 fragments of one sentence), ch14-3 (two phrases in one left, joined by "/").
- Categorization in disguise: ch4-1 (all rights mean "cautious"), ch5-1 (all rights mean "future-in-past"), AR ch6-2 (habit vs sequence, 2+2), ch21-2 (2 rights say "uses passive voice to foreground…").
- Systemic: nearly every right is a long meta-linguistic gloss that restates the left's keyword (means that → "opens a definition"; So → "draws a conclusion"; firstly → "introduces"). Learners can solve these by keyword overlap or elimination. No item asks them to produce or choose a form.
- Parity: AR ch6 (M/M/MC) and AR ch12 (M/MC/M) are ordered differently from EN. AR ch6-2 and ch13-1 carry different items from EN.
