Total matching exercises: **92** (A2 14+14, B1 19+19, B2 13+13).

Legend: MA = matching, MC = multiple-choice, FB = fill-blanks, SEQ = sequencing, RF = reflection, CF = choose-the-correct-form (inline MC), WB = gap-fill with word bank, EC = error correction, SB = sentence building (word chips), TR = transformation / sentence combining, DD = drag-drop categorisation, cMC = contextual/nuance MC.
"KEEP (reframe)" means keeping the matching interaction but making the right column sentence endings or new-context examples instead of meta-labels.

## EN/AR parity

- **B2 count 44 vs 43**: the difference is **ch4**. EN has `yu-b2-lf4-3` (fill-blanks "The tribes migrated to Anatolia to [blank] the Mongol invasion"); AR ch4 has only MA, MC and RF. Also, ch1 ordering differs (EN: MA, MC, RF, FB; AR: MA, MC, FB, RF).
- Differences in item count or content: A2 ch1 links (AR 4 / EN 3), A2 ch2 habit chunks (different items), B1 ch1 principles (different 4th item), B1 ch5 cause-response (AR «فبدأوا» vs EN "Thus, they easily defeated"), B2 ch1 (AR 4 / EN 3), B2 ch6 (while…also vs من جهة…), B2 ch9 (while/but vs في الوقت نفسه).
- AR conversions need to handle: case endings in CF distractors (منصوب after إنّ/أنّ/ولكنّ and كان's predicate), subjunctive after أن (with the ن dropped), gender agreement in word banks, right-to-left chip order in SB, and accepted answers both with and without vowels.
