# Adam A2 — Gold Reference Audit

Status: **Gold reference candidate; human final approval still required**

Blueprint: `adam-a2` v2.0.0

## Protected boundary

This migration does not edit the canonical English or Arabic story prose. Chapter order, audio, images, narration/timing and the approved story-correction mechanism remain outside the Gold pedagogy rewrite.

## Learning architecture

The 10 chapters now contain 20 explicit bilingual learning outcomes. Every outcome has observable success criteria, universal competency tags, CEFR A2 alignment, direct TYMM foreign-language reading alignment, and supporting speaking/writing or conceptual alignment only when the activity genuinely activates those skills.

All 40 authored assessment items follow the traceable chain:

`learning outcome -> chapter evidence -> assessment -> success evidence -> misconception target -> diagnostic feedback`

## A2 language rule

Cognitive demand and question-language demand are separated. An A2 learner may compare two simple ideas or connect a basic cause and result, but the question itself must remain short and concrete.

For Blueprint v2 books, a shared level-language validator now checks student-facing question and option length. A2 also rejects high-register question wording such as `according to`, `evaluate`, `analyse`, `justify`, `infer`, `implication`, `perspective`, `significance`, `synthesise`, and similar academic framing.

Adam A2 also has a manual question-language lock. Fourteen student-facing question stems were simplified after the pedagogical rewrite so the new Gold learning targets do not accidentally raise the reading level of the assessment language.

## Assessment-quality changes

Low-value detail checks were replaced where they did not represent the central learning well:

- Chapter 3 Final now checks the chapter's idea that origin/material does not itself make a person valuable.
- Chapter 4 Final checks Iblis's stated plan rather than a peripheral event detail.
- Chapter 5 Review compares learning from a mistake with refusing to admit a mistake.
- Chapter 7 Final checks how Adam and Eve guided their children rather than family quantity.
- Chapter 8 Final checks the explicit lesson about giving one's best rather than only recalling Qabil's crop amount.
- Chapter 9 Knowledge checks the move from anger to sadness/panic.
- Chapter 9 Final checks the serious consequence without making graphic detail the learning target.
- Chapter 10 Final checks the stated purpose of messenger stories rather than Adam's sadness.

Retained items remain tied to different same-chapter evidence points.

## Feedback

Every assessment has bilingual authored feedback metadata. Correct feedback confirms and explains the chapter connection. Incorrect feedback gives an item-specific rereading direction instead of simply revealing the answer.

## Vocabulary

The dedicated Vocabulary Challenge is no longer chosen by automatic even-spacing for Adam A2. Six high-value words are manually fixed:

- soil
- knowledge
- arrogant
- mistake
- offering
- jealousy

Arabic uses the reviewed paired target for each concept.

## Teacher Guide

Each chapter now includes explicit outcome coverage, prior knowledge, anticipated misconception, pre/while/post reading flow, expected responses, formative evidence, differentiation, transfer and reflection. Existing visible lesson-plan, assessment, differentiation and interactive-tip surfaces are populated from this chapter-specific Gold plan.

## Self-Study Guide

The generic study-guide path is replaced for Adam A2 with a Gold routine built around goals, evidence finding, vocabulary in context, feedback-based repair, short production/transfer, and self-check. The full guide is generated chapter by chapter from the same reviewed Blueprint.

## Remaining human gate

The system can verify structure, evidence attestation, EN/AR parity, duplicate mechanics, level-language limits, alignment completeness, and feedback wiring. It cannot replace a final human judgement about theological/source sensitivity, naturalness of every Arabic/English sentence, or whether a TYMM/CEFR alignment is pedagogically the strongest possible mapping. Those remain final-review responsibilities.
