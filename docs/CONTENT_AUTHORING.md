# Content Authoring Workflow

## Canonical input

The supplied story text is authoritative. Do not silently rewrite, correct, merge, split or reorder story prose, chapter boundaries, quotations, references or approved narration. Suspected source errors are reported separately and changed only with explicit approval.

## Authoring sequence

1. Add the exact raw EN/AR story pages and stable page ids.
2. Connect approved media paths.
3. Apply the level source/highlight locks so hotspots and Word Notes are grounded in same-chapter prose.
4. Manually author the Learning Blueprint: objectives, evidence points, vocabulary targets, questions, answers, distractors, explanations, stage eligibility, Teacher Guide and Self-Study Guide content.
5. Validate EN and AR independently against their own story prose.
6. Run the level Blueprint validator and manual language/pedagogy/history/religion review.

## Assessment contract

Every current level uses one Quick Challenge per story chapter, Knowledge 8, Review 8 and Final 10. A Vocabulary Challenge contains 6 targets at A2 or 10 at B1/B2 when the book has a dedicated vocabulary page.

Tap-Reveal is Quick-only and may appear at most twice in a book. Review is authored as exactly 4 multiple-choice + 4 true/false items and compiled to one quiz-game. Final uses scored multiple-choice, true/false, matching and fill-blanks interactions.

The same learning point must never be assessed twice across Quick, Knowledge, Review and Final.

## Level demand

- A2: direct comprehension, sequence, concrete vocabulary and short factual production.
- B1: main idea, cause/result, motivation, comparison and claim -> evidence -> explanation.
- B2: evidence selection, inference, comparison, cause, turning points and clearly qualified interpretation; writing target 120–150 words.

## Guides

Teacher and Self-Study Guides are chapter-specific Blueprint content. They must use the chapter as the evidence source, contain usable differentiation and assessment guidance, and never depend on unavailable worksheets or unverified external facts.
