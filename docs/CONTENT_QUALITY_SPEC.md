# Stories Content Quality Specification

This is the current derived-learning quality standard for every English and Arabic A2, B1 and B2 book.

## Protected boundary

Do not alter canonical story prose, chapter identity/order, approved quotations/references, narration or timing data during technical or derived-learning work. Every exercise, Word Note, hotspot and guide claim must be supported by the corresponding canonical language version.

## Blueprint ownership

Humans author the learning objectives, evidence, vocabulary targets, questions, answers, distractors, feedback and guide pedagogy. The shared engine may validate and place that material but may not invent a new claim or assessment point.

## Assessment path

- exactly one Quick Challenge per story chapter
- Knowledge Check: 8 authored items
- Review: 8 authored items, exactly 4 multiple-choice + 4 true/false, compiled to one quiz-game
- Final Challenge: 10 scored items with multiple-choice, true/false, matching and fill-blanks represented
- dedicated Vocabulary Challenge: 6 A2 or 10 B1/B2 targets when the page exists
- Tap-Reveal: Quick-only, maximum 2 per book
- one learning point may be assessed only once across the full path

Exact/near-duplicate question wording is rejected automatically; semantic repetition still requires manual review.

## Evidence and attribution

Evidence must occur in the same EN/AR chapter as the assessment point. Outside knowledge must not be required. Historical or religious uncertainty, source limits and chapter attribution must be preserved. Do not flatten phrases such as “according to sources”, “probably”, named theories or literary/Sûfî viewpoints into universal factual certainty.

Sensitive or violent material is assessed through relevant historical, causal or human consequences; derived activities must not amplify graphic details for entertainment.

## Level standard

A2 uses direct comprehension, sequence, concrete vocabulary and short factual responses. Teacher timing is 30–35 minutes; Self-Study 15–20 minutes.

B1 uses main idea, cause/result, character action or motivation, comparison and text-supported explanation. Teacher timing is 45 minutes; Self-Study 35–40 minutes.

B2 uses evidence selection, inference, comparison, cause, turning points and clearly labelled interpretation. Teacher timing is 55 minutes; Self-Study 40 minutes; analytical writing target is 120–150 words with evidence separated from interpretation and qualification.

## Feedback and distractors

Incorrect feedback must point the learner back toward useful evidence rather than merely saying “wrong” or revealing an unsupported explanation. Distractors must be plausible misunderstandings of the chapter, not newly invented religious or historical claims.

## Vocabulary and hotspots

Word Notes must occur in the same chapter and use the intended contextual meaning. Hotspot titles/descriptions follow the Story Language Lock and normally use same-chapter source wording. EN and AR are grounded independently.

## Guides

Teacher and Self-Study Guides must map to actual story chapters, use the chapter as evidence, distinguish evidence from interpretation/reflection, include differentiation and usable assessment guidance, and avoid unsupported external resources.

## Release gate

A book is release-ready only when its level Blueprint validator passes in both languages, the repository typecheck passes, and any book-specific source/qualification safeguards remain intact.
